# Visual concept review

Reviewed on 12 September 2026. Four agents each received a complete independent design brief and generated an app board and matched ad using the built-in image-generation tool. The parent agent inspected all eight final assets and the comparison page.

## What is ready

Four distinct visual directions are ready for founder review. Each has three complete screen concepts: Overview, Plan, and Purchase check. Each also has a portrait campaign concept using the same headline, subline, CTA, and disclosure. The design documents define intended typography, palette, layout, controls, chart treatment, and motion; the raster images approximate those systems.

| Direction | Most useful quality to assess | Detail to refine in an editable design |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clarity | Immediate hierarchy and precise financial rows | Give every Reserve bar an explicit denominator, or remove it. Its generated bar lengths are approximate. |
| Daylight | Warmth and editorial character without hiding the financial answer | Standardize the numeral font and add-action position between the board and ad. Increase the board footer contrast. |
| Horizon | A memorable dark palette and a clearly labelled allocation idea | Build the 88%/12% graphic from real values; verify contrast of secondary labels and preserve an accessible quiet mode. |
| Tactile | Bold hierarchy and physical-looking Reserve controls | Replace low-contrast white/orange combinations and define what the physical objects encode. Keep decorative objects distinct from quantitative charts. |

The images are concept presentations. A selected direction should be rebuilt as editable app surfaces before exact campaign exports or interaction testing. Generated icon details, device framing, and some control placement differ between boards and ads. These differences are recorded rather than treated as an approved final interface.

## Financial and copy checks

The final boards show:

- Available money: $5,000.
- Three Accounts: Everyday $3,000, Joint $1,800, Cash $200.
- Funded Reserves: Rent $1,800, Groceries $600, Bills $400, Emergency fund $1,600; total $4,400.
- Free amount: $600.
- Hypothetical purchase: $120; Free after purchase: $480.
- Purchase check ends with **Done**, with no implication that checking creates an expense.

The ads preserve the current Available money / Reserved / Free amount relationship and the Account values. Each uses “Know what’s free to spend.”, “Accounts, commitments, and your next decision.”, “Explore the concept”, and “Concept preview · Not yet available”. No ad contains fabricated testimonials, awards, or bank-integration badges.

The exact values are correct. Raster chart geometry is a visual approximation, not a validated chart implementation. No claim of tested accessibility, runtime performance, bank sync, or an operational finance app is made.

## Output dimensions

- Four app boards: 1536 × 1024 each.
- Four portrait ads: 1122 × 1402 each, approximately 4:5.

The generator did not return the larger board size or exact 1080 × 1350 requested in the briefs. No upscaling is represented as additional source detail. Exact placement exports should be produced after the chosen design is rebuilt or the generated assets are deliberately adapted.

## Suggested decision

Pick the direction whose app you want to use, then identify any detail worth borrowing from another direction. For example, liking Daylight's tone and Horizon's chart does not mean putting a cosmic background behind an editorial layout. Keep the selected system coherent.

The next creative batch can test three specific moments within that system: purchase decision, covered month, and combined Accounts. [The campaign map](CAMPAIGN-IDEAS.md) records the other hypotheses. No paid campaign was launched in this task.
