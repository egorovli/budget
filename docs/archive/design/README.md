# Sort of Budget — four visual directions

**[Open the comparison gallery](index.html)** or use the running [local preview](http://127.0.0.1:8770/). Switch between App screens and Ad concepts; choose one direction to inspect it at a larger size.

Question: which coherent app experience makes the shared financial concept desirable enough to explore?

The user selected Flighty, Crouton, Gentler Streak, Tide Guide, Lumy, Mela, Structured, (Not Boring) Habits, Speechify, and Sunlitt as positive references. Four parallel agents each received the [shared production brief](briefs/SHARED.md) and a distinct art direction.

| Direction | Character | Main references | Brief |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clarity | Precise daily instrument | Flighty, Crouton, Speechify | [Full brief](briefs/clarity.md) |
| Daylight | Warm household companion | Gentler Streak, Mela, Structured | [Full brief](briefs/daylight.md) |
| Horizon | Atmospheric, readable financial view | Tide Guide, Lumy, Sunlitt | [Full brief](briefs/horizon.md) |
| Tactile | Bold type and purposeful physical detail | Not Boring Habits, Structured, Crouton | [Full brief](briefs/tactile.md) |

Each direction produces a three-screen app board and one portrait ad. All use the same fictional Book and numbers: $5,000 available, $4,400 reserved, $600 free; a $120 purchase preview leaves $480 free. The common ad headline is “Know what’s free to spend.” This lets us compare visual direction before changing the promise.

[Campaign ideas](CAMPAIGN-IDEAS.md) maps eight promises and 24 scene/style combinations, with three suggested follow-on creatives.

These are original generated concept images, not screenshots of a working app. They are for design review and preparation of demand tests. Each direction includes its design system, exact prompts, source inspiration, and production notes. The product name remains Sort of Budget; direction names are internal labels.


## Delivered

Four app boards (1536 × 1024 each), each showing Overview, Plan, and Purchase check. Four portrait ad concepts (1122 × 1402 each, approximately 4:5). All final images use the built-in image-generation tool. Each direction has a `DESIGN.md`, exact `prompts.md`, and `result.json`.

See [review notes](review.md) for the checked financial values and the remaining differences to resolve in an editable design. Nothing was published and no campaign was launched.
