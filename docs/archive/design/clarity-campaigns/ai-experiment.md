# Your records, your choice of AI

12 September 2026. Current creative version: `clarity-usecases-v3-ai`. This is a proposed product experience shown with synthetic records. No bank, agent, permission service, or payment flow is connected.

## Product promise

Keep financial records in Budgetly. Use its ordinary screens without an agent. When useful, let your chosen agent read a selected Book, ask questions there, and check the records and assumptions behind an answer. Export records and revoke future agent access from Budgetly.

This is the product direction to test. It does not establish legal ownership terms or promise that data never leaves the device. An external agent receives selected data, and its provider's handling still matters. Revocation cannot erase records already received by another service. Offline recordkeeping remains part of the product direction; a remote agent is not an offline guarantee.

Codex and Claude are intended client examples from the brief. The creative uses a generic external-agent window, with no third-party logos or implied endorsement. We have not verified a working integration with either. MCP support varies by client and connection method; “any AI works” would overstate the evidence. Skills can explain how to use tools; they do not replace authorization or make an unverified client compatible.

## Five ways to show useful AI

| Campaign | Task before the handoff | Question in the chosen agent | Evidence visible in the answer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sync | Accounts, imported transactions, category review | Where did today's $120.70 go? | Three transactions grouped as $84.20 groceries, $12.50 dining, $24 transport |
| Envelopes | Assign the remaining $600 | What if I add $50 a week to my emergency fund? | $1,600 + 12 × $50 = $2,200; no withdrawals or interest |
| Household | Alex moves $100; Sam sees the plan | What changed in our plan today? | Groceries +$100; Everyday spending −$100; Alex at 9:41 |
| Purchase | Try $120, then $250, without saving an expense | What would a $250 purchase leave me? | $600 free − $250 = $350, with no reserve used |
| Own AI | Select the Book and read-only access | Break down today's spending for me | The three-record total, category bars, and source reference |

The savings example assumes extra future contributions. It does not claim that the current fully allocated plan can fund them. The dashed line is a scenario, not an income forecast, investment return, or guarantee. The five stories use separate sample states; the fully allocated envelope plan and the $600 free in the purchase example are not interchangeable.

## What the initial ads can establish

All five include the same AI capability, price, separate-agent-cost notice, and development disclosure. Comparing the selected campaigns can tell us which *task-led presentation of this AI-enabled product* attracts relevant, price-aware contacts under the recorded acquisition settings. The own-ai arm tests an AI-led opening and workflow. It does not measure AI's independent effect.

Do not call a good click rate proof that people will connect financial data. Ask about an actual recent task, their current agent, how they supplied records, and how they checked the answer. Invite a real read-only sample-Book task. Then observe a second question, not just the first successful connection.

A weak own-ai campaign could reflect low current agent use, unclear setup, unwillingness to share records, an adequate file-upload workaround, price, or poor distribution. A popular sync campaign could still attract users who never connect an agent. Record those reasons. Treat the common qualification definition in MEASUREMENT.md consistently across arms.

## A separate test for AI's added value

After one task earns a working trial, register a new test with two otherwise matched presentations: the task in Budgetly alone, and the same task with an optional external-agent step. Hold layout, offer, disclosure, acquisition source, performance, and form fixed as far as possible. Use a neutral acquisition message and random assignment before the page renders if the question is the page effect. Keep all assigned eligible arrivals in the denominator. This estimates presentation effect in that acquired population, not retention value or universal market demand.

For actual AI value, a later working-product trial must observe useful repeat questions, correctness against source records, trust, revocation, time/effort, and willingness to pay. Choose the effect worth detecting, baseline, sample size, error control, and stopping rule before that test. The existing $600 pilot is exploratory; do not buy an extra AI/no-AI matrix from the same small budget.

## First integration that a promising result earns

One client and one documented connection method. A synthetic Book. Explicit read-only scopes. Exact record totals and traceable references. A scenario with named assumptions. Clear provider data sharing. Expiry and revocation checks. No agent writes in this first trial. Add write workflows only through a separate product decision with explicit user review.

## Sources and limits

The [MCP authorization specification](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2025-11-25/basic/authorization) describes authorization requirements for HTTP transports. It supports designing controlled access; it does not certify our proposed scopes, screen, or client compatibility. The [campaign method note](../research/campaign-experiment-method.md) contains primary evidence on delivery bias, funnel denominators, and uncertainty. Product positioning, sample amounts, test order, and the $600 pilot are our proposals, not findings from those sources.
