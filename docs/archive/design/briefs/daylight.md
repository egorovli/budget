## Art direction

Create a warm, sophisticated personal finance experience, like a beautifully designed daily journal with excellent information hierarchy. Warm white paper, deep brown-black ink, apricot and soft tomato accents, a quiet lavender or blue secondary tone. Combine a restrained editorial serif for a few human headings with a clean highly readable sans for numbers and controls. Generous but useful space. Thin rules, compact rows, one or two softly tinted areas. Avoid generic sage-green budgeting UI. It must feel adult, original, tactile and welcoming.

Overview: a gentle short context line (e.g. “Room for today”), then the clear Free amount $600. A small human illustration or sun-like motif can appear at the edge, but the financial answer stays dominant. A household cue can use two tasteful initials/avatar circles. Plan: dated commitments in a calm vertical rhythm, each Reserve's name and amount clear, with subtle colored markers. Purchase check: a large amount and reassuring, precise after-purchase result with a clear action. Stable bottom navigation, comfortable input controls.

The ad should use warm paper and confident editorial type with the phone rendered large and flat enough to inspect. Small original illustration may humanize it; no stock smiling couple. Promise territory: a shared plan with less second-guessing. Secondary future hooks: “A money plan you can both see.” and “Know what’s covered. Enjoy what’s left.” Do not add those to the required matched ad.

Distinctness test: warm, humane, editorial, composed. No infantilizing mascot, no fake optimism about a negative balance, no heavy financial-terminal look, no cosmic gradient.
