## Art direction

Create a bold, memorable but credible financial app with physical-feeling Reserve objects and beautiful oversized type. Warm off-white and near-black with one vivid orange or yellow accent; use restrained lilac only if it supports the system. High contrast, confident proportions, deliberate asymmetric composition. A dimensional tile, stack, disc, or slot can visualize setting money aside, but labels and values must stay conventional and legible. This is not a gaming economy, crypto app, 3D token marketplace, or endless bento grid.

Overview: large $600 Free amount, clearly related to $5,000 Available money and $4,400 Reserved. One sculptural shape can emphasize the money set aside, supported by a quiet exact label. Plan: four tactile but compact Reserve rows/tiles, all values visible; the objects should suggest allocation and control, not coins that need to be counted. Purchase check: oversized $120 input, crisp $480 after value, a decisive primary control and familiar secondary route. Consistent minimal bottom navigation. Pair a bold modern grotesk heading with excellent numeral forms and small quiet metadata. A physical-looking control must still look usable.

The ad should have an arresting near-black/cream/orange composition and a dominant phone. One striking object or typographic move gives it identity; app content supplies the evidence. Promise territory: give money a purpose and make the next decision satisfying. Secondary future hooks: “Put your money where your plans are.” and “Make room for what matters.” Do not add those to the required matched ad.

Distinctness test: this should be the most opinionated and tactile direction, with personality extending to real screens. No cloned checkmark mascot from Not Boring, no gratuitous confetti, no slow animations implied on every expense.
