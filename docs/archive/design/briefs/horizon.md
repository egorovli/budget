## Art direction

Create an immersive but exceptionally legible finance app that treats the month as a readable horizon. Deep midnight indigo, ink blue, softly illuminated periwinkle, peach/gold accent. Rich but controlled atmospheric gradient only where it communicates the hero area. Use fine time scales, smooth chart geometry, crisp small labels, large beautiful numerals. It should feel like a precision weather or celestial app applied thoughtfully to financial planning. Avoid neon cyberpunk, glass panels everywhere, stock aurora wallpaper, or invented investment graphs.

Overview must retain a large $600 Free amount and exact smaller Available money $5,000 / Reserved $4,400. A restrained horizon or segmented ring can visualize reserved versus free parts of the same $5,000 (88% reserved, 12% free), labelled so it is not an invented balance forecast. Plan: a month/date rhythm and four Reserve rows with real values; no fake daily series. Purchase check: amount $120 with Free after purchase $480, using a luminous but quiet visual shift. Numbers remain deterministic. If a chart is purely decorative, do not give it financial axis labels. Prefer a clearly labelled allocation graphic that encodes the given values.

The ad should be a dramatic midnight editorial poster with a luminous phone screen and excellent contrast. Keep the headline readable, the $600 dominant in the phone, and the composition calm. Promise territory: seeing commitments before they surprise you. Secondary future hooks: “See the month before it happens.” and “A clearer view of your next decision.” Do not add those to the required matched ad.

Distinctness test: this is the atmospheric data direction. Rich color, meaningful chart, exact values, carefully graded light. Not the white calculator or warm journal.
