## Question

Within individuals and households, which specific situation, current workaround, and unresolved problem should the first concept address? What useful outcome would make someone consider changing their current behavior? Distinguish observed problems from assumptions, and a user's financial problem from their interest in AI.

## Answer

Resolved with the founder on 2026-09-12. The founder selected all three proposed needs together: keeping several Accounts current, planning shared spending, and understanding what remains available after commitments. They asked to proceed with something concrete.

Treat these as a connected workflow for individuals and households: maintain the financial picture, plan the use of money, and use that plan for everyday spending decisions. Do not force the founder to select just one of these product capabilities before drafting a concept.

This is a founder-selected problem hypothesis. It is not evidence that a particular customer segment has demand. Current workarounds, problem frequency, household access needs, acquisition targeting, and willingness to pay remain unverified.

The next discussion will use [Budgetly — product concept 0.1](../concept-v0.md), a provisional brief and four-screen storyboard. The draft is an input to the next decision, not an accepted product specification.
