# Focus Clarity campaigns on four useful jobs

Type: research
Label: wayfinder:research
Status: resolved
Parent: ../map.md

## Question

After the founder selected Clarity, which distinct promises and screen demonstrations should the next demand experiment prepare, and how can the results guide a small product trial?

## Answer

The founder selected Clarity as the sole active visual direction on 12 September 2026. Prepare four job stories: bank sync, envelope allocation, transaction retrieval, and a purchase check. Use one visual system and a consistent proposed offer. The two allocation states must remain distinct: fully assigned $5,000 with $600 available in an envelope, versus $4,400 reserved and $600 Free amount.

The [active campaign package](../../../docs/design-experiments/2026-09-concepts/clarity-campaigns/README.md) contains four ads, four app-screen concepts, matching page previews, source research, copy/motion briefs, and a measurement handoff. The [experiment plan](../../../docs/design-experiments/2026-09-concepts/clarity-campaigns/EXPERIMENT-PLAN.md) owns the current starting order and four-cell $600 media proposal, replacing the older three-cell split. These remain preparation assumptions, not authorization or evidence.

Measure qualified price-aware interest, then observed tasks and reasons to switch. Research found existing product features, not an unmet market. Bank sync must not imply universal instant updates. Production domain details and delivery feasibility remain open. No campaign, collection, spend or production application was launched.
