# Prepare the demand experiment kit for delegation

Type: task
Label: wayfinder:task
Status: resolved
Assignee: codex
Parent: ../map.md

## Question

Prepare a thorough, finite catalogue of meaningful product promise, audience, format, channel, and offer combinations, with prioritized creative briefs and explicit instructions another model can execute. Include attractive design direction, screenshots and Remotion video storyboards, landing-page copy, a test sequence, data collection, economics, decision rules, and completion checks. Use labelled defaults for unresolved choices so the founder need not answer routine questions.

The founder requested preparation directly on 2026-09-12. This task is independent of closing the earlier human decision tickets. Outputs are proposals for experiments, not validated product decisions, implemented integrations, or launched campaigns.

## Answer

Prepared on 2026-09-12: [Budgetly demand experiment kit](../demand-kit/START-HERE.md). It includes a finite 20-promise catalogue, 7 audiences, 9 formats, 8 channel/delivery contexts and 4 offers, exhaustively enumerated with compatibility rules. The delivered map contains 40,320 combinations: 4,503 compatible recipes and 35,817 exclusions with reasons. Twenty-six written briefs cover every promise and specify a small starting set.

The kit provides exact copy, scene instructions and Remotion frame timing, a shared visual system, synthetic financial data, matching-page copy, proposed offers and pricing, market/channel guidance, a staged budget and decision plan, event and result templates, scripts, and eight handoff assignments. Dated primary-source research supports channel constraints, tooling and competitor-price context. Defaults are proposals chosen for preparation, not founder-approved spending or validated business choices.

The catalogue generator can produce further briefs from compatible combinations. Validation checked counts, unique identities, all-promise coverage, local links, CSV structure, financial sample arithmetic, reporting edge cases, extra-brief generation, offer-specific routes and invalid-combination rejection. No ads, pages, videos, customer messages or paid offers were published or launched by this task.
