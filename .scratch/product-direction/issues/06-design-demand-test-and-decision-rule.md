## Question

What hypothesis, offer, user action, price signal if appropriate, budget, duration, sample constraints, and follow-up evidence make the first experiment useful? Define in advance which outcomes support building a small working slice, revising the audience or promise, or stopping. Separate interest, willingness to pay, usability, feasibility, and retention. Explain inconclusive and negative results without treating one failed advertisement as proof of no demand. Produce the experiment brief for later execution.

## Answer

Resolved with the founder on 2026-10-21: no demand test now. The product is for the founder's own use, so the first evidence is the founder's weekly use. We return to a demand test if we decide to show the product to other people. The blocking tickets 02, 04 and 05 are archived and do not apply now. See [Decisions of 2026-10-21](../../../docs/product.md#decisions-of-2026-10-21).
