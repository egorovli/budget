# Define the product promise and core experience

Type: grilling
Label: wayfinder:grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01
Parent: ../../../docs/product.md

## Question

For the selected user problem, what outcome does Sort of Budget promise and what short workflow makes that promise concrete? Which of bank synchronization, budgeting, reports, offline use, and existing-agent access are necessary to that promise? Define what a concept must show and identify uncertain feasibility claims for later focused research.

## Discussion asset

[Sort of Budget — product concept 0.1](../../../docs/archive/product-direction/concept-v0.md) is a proposed brief and four-screen storyboard prepared after the founder selected the combined problem. Its numbers are synthetic and its design choices need review. This ticket remains open.

## Answer

Resolved with the founder on 2026-10-21. We build Sort of Budget for the founder's own use first and research what can be connected to it. There is no external promise. The measure of success: the founder uses the app every week and trusts its numbers.

Bank synchronization, planning, reports and agent access are not necessary for this measure. Offline use is necessary because it is a project principle. The first slice tests the core of the idea: the Free amount ([issue 10](10-build-the-free-amount-slice.md)). Statement import follows ([issue 12](12-import-statement-history.md)). The feasibility of live bank synchronization goes to [issue 11](11-research-bank-synchronization.md). See [Decisions of 2026-10-21](../../../docs/product.md#decisions-of-2026-10-21).

## Comments

2026-10-21: The first answer selected an import of statement history as the first slice. The founder changed this on the same day: an import alone does not test the idea. The first slice is now the Free amount.
