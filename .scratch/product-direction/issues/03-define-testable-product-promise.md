## Question

For the selected user problem, what outcome does Budgetly promise and what short workflow makes that promise concrete? Which of bank synchronization, budgeting, reports, offline use, and existing-agent access are necessary to that promise? Define what a concept must show and identify uncertain feasibility claims for later focused research.
